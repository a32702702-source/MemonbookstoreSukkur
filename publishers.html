Publishers & Textbook Boards

Memon Book Store carries official Sindh Textbook Board textbooks alongside three leading private educational publishers. Their details below come from their own official websites and curriculum catalogues.

Publisher STBB Jamshoro

Sindh Textbook Board

Jamshoro, Sindh, Pakistan

Sindh Textbook Board (STBB) Jamshoro is the provincial educational authority responsible for developing, standardizing and publishing curriculum textbooks from Class 1 through Intermediate (Class 12). Its curriculum is prescribed across all BISE boards in Sindh (Sukkur, Karachi, Hyderabad, Larkana, Mirpurkhas, Shaheed Benazirabad) in English, Urdu and Sindhi mediums.
